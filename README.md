## About

Alexander Maslov — thermal engineer, developer, and designer. I work at the intersection of physics and software: modeling thermal systems, then building the tools that run them.

- 🎓 VSTU — Thermal Power Engineering (2022–2026) → MEPhI — Nuclear Physics & Technology (2026–2028)
- 🏭 Hands-on experience at Kursk NPP-2, SKIF (Novosibirsk), SIBUR, and Novovoronezh NPP
- 🔭 Currently building digital twins of thermal systems, from physics to implementation
- 🌐 [mslv.space](https://mslv.space) · [CV](https://mslv.space/cv)
